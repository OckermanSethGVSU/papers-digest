# Vector Search Digest — 2026-11-24

## Highlights

- **[Look Before You Leap: Re-Architecting Disk-Resident Vector Search with TriVS](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29948)** — An I/O-centric breakdown of disk ANN into approach and converge phases leads to a hybrid design: IVF routing, an in-memory quantized distill step, then vector-level SSD reads, at 3.4x PipeANN's throughput with less memory, which directly challenges the pure-graph DiskANN design.
- **[Zarc: Exact Nearest Neighbors over a Billion Vectors in Seconds](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29252)** — Exact kNN over 1B vectors in about 1 second for 10K queries on 4 GPUs, using low-precision pruning proofs kept on GPU and full vectors in host memory, with filters and updates, which resets the bar any approximate index must beat.

## ANN index architecture: disk, GPU, multi-component, sparse

### [Look Before You Leap: Re-Architecting Disk-Resident Vector Search with TriVS](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29948)
_Yizou Chen, Baotong Lu, Peiqi Yin et al. | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.IR) | ⭐⭐⭐_

The paper splits disk ANN queries into an approach phase and a converge phase. Cluster indexes avoid approach I/O but read whole clusters, while graph indexes read precisely but pay I/O just to get near the query. TriVS routes coarsely through in-memory centroids, distills the routed clusters into a shortlist with compact quantized codes, then fetches exact vectors at vector granularity, reaching 3.42x PipeANN throughput at Recall@10=0.9 with 63.7% less I/O.

**Vector search angle:** For SSD-resident indexes, the takeaway is to coarsen IVF partitions to shrink the centroid index, spend the freed DRAM on PQ/RaBitQ codes for an in-memory filtering stage, and issue random reads only for the final rerank.

### [Zarc: Exact Nearest Neighbors over a Billion Vectors in Seconds](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29252)
_Dongfang Zhao | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.IR) | ⭐⭐⭐_

Zarc extends a GPU method that skips most vectors via cheap half-precision distance-bound proofs to datasets larger than one GPU. It keeps only short proof rows in HBM and fetches the few surviving full vectors from host memory. It supports L2, cosine, inner-product and filtered queries exactly under updates, answering 10K queries over 1B vectors in 1.1s on 4 H100s, and also speeds up exact top-k attention-key retrieval.

**Vector search angle:** Bound-based pruning with low-precision prefixes in fast memory and full precision in slow memory is a tiered exact-search design you could adopt for reranking, ground-truth generation, or exact filtered fallbacks.

### [SJG: A Skyline Joint Graph for Multi-Component Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29399)
_Linyun Ma, Rui Mao | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.DB) | ⭐⭐⭐_

This work targets graph ANN where the distance is a weighted sum over several vector components and the weights are known only at query time. The key observation is that candidate dominance across components does not depend on the weights. SJG therefore pools candidates across components and keeps each node's skyline as its edges, giving up to 27.9x QPS at 90% recall, up to 201x faster single-threaded builds, and an index at least 36% smaller than the smallest baseline.

**Vector search angle:** Skyline-based edge pruning is a principled replacement for RNG/alpha pruning in multi-vector or weighted hybrid search where query-time weights vary, with one graph instead of per-field indexes plus result merging.

### [The abc index: Exact Retrieval for Learned Sparse Representations](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28555)
_Rossano Venturini | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.IR) | ⭐⭐⭐_

abc is an exact learned-sparse-retrieval index built from term-at-a-time evaluation, Block-Max MaxScore, binary packing and doc-id reordering, engineered for modern CPUs. It is 6.5-84x faster than Qdrant's exact sparse search and 2.9-18x faster than BMP. Against approximate indexes (Seismic, HNSW) it uses 1.5-3.6x less space and overtakes them by up to 7.4x at k=1000.

**Vector search angle:** This is a direct benchmark and a design blueprint for the sparse-vector side of hybrid engines: exact, parameter-free sparse search can beat approximate sparse indexes at large k.

### [PROSPER: LLMs as Sparse Retrievers for First-Stage Product Search](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28645)
_Hongru Song, Yu-An Liu, Ruqing Zhang et al. | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.IR) | ⭐_

PROSPER is an LLM-based learned sparse retriever for e-commerce. A literal residual network keeps brand and model terms from being washed out by expansion, and a top-k lexical focusing window sparsifies early in training. It improves offline metrics and online A/B revenue in a hybrid retrieval stack.

**Vector search angle:** Top-k sparsification bounds posting-list growth from LLM-vocabulary expansion, which affects sparse index size and the latency of the sparse leg in hybrid search.

## Clustering and vector quantization techniques

### [Fuzzy e-Means: Fuzzy c-Means on Large Data with Moving-Average Mini-Batch Updates](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29241)
_Paritosh Tiwari, Punit Rathore | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

The paper ports e-means' moving-average mini-batch optimizer, with batch and step sizes set by scaling laws, to fuzzy c-means. It warm-starts with one hard e-means pass and then runs fuzzy passes without centroid relocation. It matches 25 alternating-optimization iterations in 2-4 passes, 11-20x faster on 1M-point embedding sets with 1,024 clusters, scaling to 109.5M points and 16K clusters.

**Vector search angle:** Soft memberships at scale are a cheap way to choose spill or redundant assignments for IVF and to build overlapping partitions, and the paper's note that m=2 collapses centroids in high dimensions is a useful warning.

### [Orthogonally Mixed Sign Coding with Progressive Residual Refinement for Transformer Weight Compression](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31089)
_Anindya Roy Chowdhury, Atanu Barai, Nandakishore Santhi | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.IR) | ⭐⭐_

This is a training-free, codebook-free quantizer that applies a randomized orthogonal rotation to Gaussianize residuals. It then encodes each stage as signs with an analytically calibrated scale, refining residuals progressively. Five stages bring GPT-2 Large and Llama-3-8B close to full-precision perplexity, and ablations show the rotation matters substantially.

**Vector search angle:** Rotation plus multi-stage residual sign codes is essentially a progressive RaBitQ / residual binary quantizer, usable for embeddings that need variable-precision reranking without trained codebooks.

### [One Million Tokens in 20GB: Calibration-Free 2-Bit SRFT KV-Cache Compression for Looped MoE Inference on Apple Silicon](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29092)
_Mohamed Amine Bergach | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.PF) | ⭐⭐_

The method applies a Subsampled Randomized Fourier Transform to KV vectors and then uses fixed Lloyd-Max scalar quantization at about 2 bits per coordinate, with no calibration. A custom Metal kernel with a 256-entry LUT decodes at 73.4 GB/s. The result is 1M-token inference in under 20 GB on an Apple Mac mini with small perplexity loss.

**Vector search angle:** A fast structured rotation followed by a fixed Gaussian Lloyd-Max grid gives data-independent scalar quantization for embeddings, and the LUT-based decode kernel transfers to Apple/ARM ADC scans.

### [BitShare: Sensitivity-Aware Binning and Bit Sharing for Compacting LLM Weight Storage](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28607)
_Qiuyu Ren, Sudipta Paria, Aritra Dasgupta et al. | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐_

BitShare is a group-wise integer weight format in which less sensitive channels share their low-order bits within a group. It chooses stored values with a sharing-aware GPTQ and needs no codebook. At 2-3 bits it beats GPTQ, SliM-LLM and GPTAQ on perplexity, or stores 13.6-19.4% fewer bits at equal quality.

**Vector search angle:** Shared low bits across less-important dimensions is a cheap mixed-precision scalar quantization idea that could squeeze SQ codes for embeddings with uneven per-dimension variance.

### [Estimating Silenced Tokens in Quantized LLMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29536)
_Giuseppe Pappalardo | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.CL) | ⭐_

The paper certifies which vocabulary rows can never be the argmax of the output layer after quantization. It combines witness directions, an upper bound on how close a row is to being trapped inside the others, and exact matches to get a certified count range. Silencing appears even at 8 bits on large vocabularies and falls disproportionately on non-Latin tokens.

**Vector search angle:** Reachability under argmax inner product is the MIPS question of which points lie on the convex hull, so these certificates hint at measuring how quantization makes database vectors unretrievable as top-1.

### [Randomized Weighted Distance Nearest Neighbor Classification](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29720)
_Shayan Shafaei, Naeem Shahabi Sani, Chao Lan | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐_

This work randomizes the greedy densest-homogeneous-ball search in weighted-distance NN data compression. That cuts compression time to 0.1-1% of the original method with similar accuracy. Consistency and a generalization bound are retained.

**Vector search angle:** Randomized ball covering is a cheap prototype or coreset selection for shrinking labeled kNN stores, but it is classification-focused rather than retrieval-focused.

## Sparse attention and KV cache as top-k vector retrieval

### [RefineKV: Query-Adaptive KV Cache Compression via Attention-Error Bounds](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28171)
_Kewei Lian, Jiayi Zhai, Anji Liu | 2026-11-20 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

RefineKV clusters the KV cache and, per query, either uses a cluster's compact representative or expands the cluster to its exact entries. The choice comes from an attention-error upper bound that combines cluster importance and representation distortion. It gives up to 7.6x attention speedup near full-attention quality.

**Vector search angle:** The importance x distortion criterion is a principled rule for deciding which IVF lists to scan exactly versus score by centroid or compressed code, i.e. an adaptive nprobe/rerank policy.

### [Near-Far Attention: Region-Aware Sparse Retrieval for Long-Context LLM Decoding](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30554)
_YuanShuo Wang, Jianchao Tan, Lei Gong et al. | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

NFA marks skippable context regions during prefill. It then scores the remaining pages and tokens with calibration-free subspace features using a two-level, distance-aware selector under a shared budget. It reaches up to 2.97x decode speedup at 512K tokens on Qwen3-8B.

**Vector search angle:** Two-level page-then-token selection on shared low-dimensional projected features is a coarse-to-fine top-k pattern analogous to IVF probing plus reranking on reduced dimensions.

### [Position-Skipped Distillation of Sparse-Attention Indexers: Train Short, Serve Long](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29664)
_Fu Dai, Haodi Quan, Kamal Taha | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

The authors trace the failure of short-trained sparse-attention indexers at long contexts mainly to RoPE, and fix it with gapped position IDs during distillation, cutting training compute about 6x. They also quantize indexer keys to 3-4 bits without retraining. A fused integer kernel scans them 1.6-2.2x faster than half precision.

**Vector search angle:** Post-hoc 4-bit key quantization with a fused integer scoring kernel and negligible top-k loss is another data point for int4 brute-force or ADC scans as a first-stage filter.

### [DisSparse: Pipelined Top-p Sparse Attention for Long-Context LLM Serving](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28522)
_Nurlan Nazaraliyev, Elaheh Sadredini, Elaheh Sadredini et al. | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

DisSparse integrates top-p block selection into vLLM. It pipelines selection off the critical path, uses a single-pass histogram top-p kernel, and relocates tokens so that important and unimportant tokens are not mixed in the same KV block. It is 1.17-1.80x faster at block selection and supports up to 8x larger batches than dense vLLM.

**Vector search angle:** A histogram-based single-pass threshold selection kernel and the intra-block sparsity relocation idea map to GPU top-k selection and to co-locating hot vectors in IVF lists or disk pages.

### [ANOR: Scaling Sparse Attention with a GPU-PNM Computing Hierarchy](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30417)
_Hyunsu Ye, Hyunmin Choi, Jaehong Cho et al. | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.AR) | ⭐⭐_

ANOR keeps the full KV cache on CXL processing-near-memory devices and caches recently selected entries on the GPU. The PNM computes only the misses, and cache updates are bounded and decoupled from the current step. This gives 2.3x decode throughput over HiSparse.

**Vector search angle:** Treating CXL-PNM as a compute tier that serves cache misses, with locality-aware GPU caching, is a template for tiered vector search where hot vectors sit in HBM and cold distance computations run near memory.

### [IndexerStage: Staging Indexer-K across Memory Tiers for Sparse-Attention LLM Serving](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28625)
_Xin Zhang, Weifeng Zhang, Xianyan Xie et al. | 2026-11-21 | arXiv (cs.DC) | ⭐_

IndexerStage externalizes DeepSeek Sparse Attention's per-layer indexer keys to backing GPUs and streams pages layer by layer through shared buffers. Admission control accounts for both memory and per-layer transfer demand. It raises batch size by 12-23% and decode throughput by up to 1.31x.

**Vector search angle:** Streaming per-layer scan state with joint memory-and-bandwidth admission control is relevant to brute-force or flat-scan serving where the scanned vectors exceed accelerator memory.

### [Fast 4-bit KV-Cache Decoding on Hopper GPUs: Why Existing Kernels Are Slow and How to Fix Them](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30027)
_Jian Zhang | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

Instruction-count profiling shows that existing 4-bit KV kernels on Hopper are instruction-issue bound and serialize loading, decoding and matrix multiplies. The fix is a TMA producer warp overlapped with compute warpgroups, a write-time layout that feeds decoded values from registers to tensor cores, and operand swapping, reaching 67-75% of peak bandwidth. K mean removal and rotation preserve accuracy.

**Vector search angle:** This is directly useful for GPU int4/PQ distance kernels: software dequantization becomes the bottleneck, and write-time layout plus warp specialization recovers memory bandwidth.

### [Gossamer: A Utility-Driven Architecture for Constant-Budget KV Cache Compression in Reasoning LLMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29497)
_Feng Cheng, Cong Guo, Junyao Zhang et al. | 2026-11-22 | arXiv (cs.AR) | ⭐_

Gossamer is a hardware co-design that picks quantization, merging, or eviction per KV entry under a fixed budget. It fuses cosine-similarity estimation into idle attention compute cycles. Throughput improves up to 16.7x over uncompressed baselines.

**Vector search angle:** The link is tangential: merging near-duplicate vectors under a fixed memory budget resembles deduplication and compaction policies for streaming vector stores.

## Embedding models affecting what is indexed

### [TowerBridge: Bridging the Dual-Encoder Modality Gap via Weight-Space Programming](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31233)
_Hani Alomari, Md Atabuzzaman, Chris Thomas | 2026-11-23 | arXiv (cs.LG) | ⭐⭐_

A paired statistic identifies directions where CLIP's two towers agree more on hard negatives than on matched pairs. A closed-form weight edit that mildly attenuates these directions is computed in seconds from 25K labelled pairs. It improves COCO and Flickr30k retrieval across 21 checkpoints and reduces hubness on all of them.

**Vector search angle:** Reduced hubness and a closed-form linear correction could be applied as a projection on stored embeddings, which may improve graph-index connectivity and cross-modal recall without re-encoding through the model.

### [DiLE: Discrimination-Aware Latent-Space Learning for Universal Multimodal Embeddings](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25419)
_Ben Kang, Junda Wang, Yuxuan Yuan et al. | 2026-11-19 | HuggingFace | ⭐_

DiLE trains multimodal LLM embedders that use learnable queries to build a compact latent space. Training combines shared-private expert adaptation with on-policy hard-negative distillation, where candidates are mined by refreshed student snapshots. It reaches state of the art on MMEB-v2, UVRB and MRMR.

**Vector search angle:** On-policy hard negatives mined from the student's own retrieval index tie embedding training to the ANN layer's actual confusions, which matters when indexed embeddings are periodically refreshed.
